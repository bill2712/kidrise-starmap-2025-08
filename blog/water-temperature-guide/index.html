The Importance of Water Temperature

Why water temperature matters just as much as your beans, and how to control it.

2026-05-27

The Boiling Point Myth

A common misconception is that you need boiling water (100°C / 212°F) to brew coffee. For specialty light roasts, boiling water can sometimes be beneficial, but for medium to dark roasts, it will easily over-extract and lead to harsh, ashy flavors.

The ideal range for most specialty coffee lies between 90°C and 96°C (195°F - 205°F).

How to Control It

  1. Use a Gooseneck Kettle with Variable Temp: This is the best investment you can make for pour-over coffee.
  2. The “Wait” Method: If you only have a standard kettle, bring it to a boil and then let it sit off the boil with the lid off for about 30 to 45 seconds before pouring.

Temperature stability throughout the brew also matters. Pre-heating your dripper and decanter is essential to prevent rapid temperature loss.
