The Ultimate Guide to Dialing in Grind Size

Mastering grind size is the single most important factor in dialing in your specialty coffee. Here's how to get it right every time.

2026-05-28

Understanding Extraction

When water meets coffee, it acts as a solvent. The rate at which the water dissolves the delicious compounds in your coffee is largely determined by the surface area of the coffee grounds. Finer grounds mean more surface area and faster extraction, while coarser grounds mean less surface area and slower extraction.

  • Too Fine (Over-extracted): Bitter, harsh, astringent, dry finish.
  • Too Coarse (Under-extracted): Sour, acidic, salty, quick finish.
  • Just Right: Sweet, balanced, complex, lingering pleasant finish.

Finding the Sweet Spot

Start with the recommended setting for your brewing method. For a V60 pour-over, this is typically medium-fine. Brew a cup, and taste it. Your palate is the ultimate judge.

  1. If it tastes sour, go finer.
  2. If it tastes bitter, go coarser.

Keep a tasting log (or mental notes) and adjust by small increments. Happy brewing!
